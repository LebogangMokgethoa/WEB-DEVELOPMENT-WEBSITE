Paws Shelter is a community-based non-profit organisation enstablished to rescue, shelter, and rehome stray,abandoned, and surrendered animals within the local area. Since our funding, we have relied on comunninty donation and volunteer support we provide veterinary care, food, and safe housing to animals in neeed.
